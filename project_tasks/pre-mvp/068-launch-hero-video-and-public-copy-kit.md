## Why this story exists

The current 58-second app recording is close to a strong launch asset. GrokForge’s product promise is now visible: normal tools see one folder, while GrokForge works across a whole project with multi-root context, agent chat, voice, files, and a polished desktop shell.

This story turns that raw recording into a launch-ready hero video and a small copy kit that can be reused for the X launch thread, landing page, captions, and release notes.

## Goals

- Produce a 45–55 second hero video from the current app footage or a fresh equivalent recording.
- Add subtle narration or caption beats that explain the multi-root/project-first value.
- End with a polished visual moment, likely Settings -> Appearance theme switching.
- Draft reusable public copy for launch surfaces.
- Keep claims grounded in working functionality.

## Suggested video structure

- 0–5s: “Normal tools see one folder.”
- 5–15s: Show multi-root workspace with LifeOS and Chatterbox side by side.
- 15–28s: Show unified agent thread and project-aware context.
- 28–38s: Show file/editor flow, diff/edit/review if stable enough.
- 38–48s: Show voice/read-aloud or voice indicator if stable enough.
- 48–55s: Close on Settings theme switcher or recent projects polish.

## Copy kit scope

- X launch thread draft.
- Hero headline and subheadline.
- Three to five feature bullets.
- Short product description for README/release notes.
- Caption script or voiceover script with timestamps.

## Open questions

- Should the launch video include voice mode, or avoid it unless full-duplex voice is highly reliable in the launch build?
- Should the video show file writes/diff review, or keep the first launch video focused on project context and navigation?
- Should the ending visual be theme switching, recent projects, or a successful agent answer?
- Should narration be spoken voiceover, text captions, or both?
- Should public copy say “Grok-native,” “xAI-powered,” or avoid platform-specific claims until positioning is finalized?
- Where will the primary landing page live, and does it need product screenshots or just the hero video?

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Hero video is trimmed to 45–55 seconds or the chosen duration is documented.
- [ ] Video has captions, voiceover, or an equivalent text treatment.
- [ ] Copy kit includes X launch thread, hero copy, feature bullets, and short product description.
- [ ] Claims in copy match implemented launch functionality.
- [ ] Final assets are documented with paths or links in the story notes.
